The practical guide

Turn Kakobuy finds into a usable shortlist.

A Kakobuy shopping guide should help you make a comparison, not just collect links. Keep a small number of relevant options and make the missing information visible.

  1. Choose one category and a purpose.

    Decide whether you need everyday sneakers, a warmer layer, sportswear or an accessory. Set a budget before comparing individual listings.

  2. Save the original listing details.

    Keep the seller name, product link, selected variation, listed currency and the date you checked. Treat prices and stock as changeable.

  3. Compare measurable attributes.

    For a Kakobuy clothing spreadsheet, compare garment measurements. For footwear, use the relevant size chart and any available internal-length information.

  4. Check the total before paying.

    Review shipping estimates, service charges, return conditions and applicable import costs. Confirm current platform rules and item eligibility with the provider you intend to use.

Questions, answered plainly

Before you follow a product link.

Short answers for a more informed first step.

What is a Kakobuy spreadsheet?

A Kakobuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize shopping links and comparison notes. Fields can include category, seller, item price, size information and photographs. A listing alone does not verify authenticity, availability or seller reliability.

Is this the official Kakobuy website?

No. This is an independent editorial directory. Its browsing links lead to CAFANS, and it does not provide Kakobuy account access, order processing or official support.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the seller’s garment measurements with a similar item you already own. Check units, measurement methods and any stated tolerance instead of relying on a size label alone.

Does a product photograph prove authenticity?

No. Photographs can help assess visible details, but they do not independently prove authenticity, seller authorization or the condition of the item that will be delivered.

What costs should I check before buying?

Check the item price, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties. Confirm current amounts and terms with the relevant provider before paying.
